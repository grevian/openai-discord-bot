How to Ask Danbot for Things

I'll probably get it wrong, but here is how to ask anyway.

Chat

Mention me in Discord: @Danbot <anything>. I will tell you a long story that goes nowhere. Add 🧵 to your message and I will start a thread, where I remember what we said. You still have to @Danbot me every time, even inside the thread.

Draw

@Danbot 🎨 <idea> or @Danbot draw me a picture of <idea>. Stick figures, wax crayon, 1024x1024. The arms are supposed to be like that. Your drawing shows up in the gallery.

Edit

Attach an image to your message, or reply to one of my own drawings, and say one of:

  • @Danbot ✏️ <change>
  • @Danbot edit: <change>
  • @Danbot redraw <change>

The picture has to be png, jpeg or webp, and under 50MB. The result is 1024x1024. If you forget the picture I will say so, sadly.

Summarise a link

Send @Danbot <https link> on its own, or @Danbot 📰 <link> or @Danbot summarize <link>. I read the page (well, Kagi reads it for me) and give you one paragraph about it, from its first 12,000 characters. Only https links. A link in the middle of a normal message is just chat.

Help

Say @Danbot help on its own and I will list all of this again. "help me write a poem" is just chat.

Sad limits

  • Some ideas get blocked by OpenAI's content policy. Try rewording.
  • If I am busy or broken, try again in a minute.
  • The gallery keeps the newest 48 drawings only.
